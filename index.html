AI WhatsApp Automation — n8n + Gemini

End-to-end n8n automation: receives WhatsApp messages → Gemini AI generates personalized reply → logs to 100+ leads auto-classified & routed to WhatsApp without manual work. → sends auto-reply. Runs 24/7 with zero human input.

Workflow Architecture
  • Node 1: WhatsApp Trigger — receives customer messages
  • Node 2: If/Else — smart routing (new vs returning)
  • Node 3: Gemini AI — generates personalized contextual reply
  • Node 4: Google Sheets — logs full conversation to CRM
  • Node 5: WhatsApp Send — auto-replies to customer

  • Every conversation automatically recorded as a CRM lead
