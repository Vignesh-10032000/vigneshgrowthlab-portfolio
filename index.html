🟢 Live & Running

⚠️ The Problem

NextGen Toys operated solely from their physical retail showroom. Customers had to visit or call to check stock availability for RC drones, helicopters, and ride-on bikes. 🚀 The Results

Delivered a fully operational online store representing their complete showroom inventory. Customers can now browse, compare models, read authentic reviews, and place orders directly with digital receipts sent automatically via WhatsApp.

AI WhatsApp Automation — n8n + Gemini

End-to-end n8n automation: receives WhatsApp messages → Gemini AI generates personalized reply → logs to Google Sheets CRM → sends auto-reply. Runs 24/7 with zero human input.

Workflow Architecture
  • Node 1: WhatsApp Trigger — receives customer messages
  • Node 2: If/Else — smart routing (new vs returning)
  • Node 3: Gemini AI — generates personalized contextual reply
  • Node 4: Google Sheets — logs full conversation to CRM
  • Node 5: WhatsApp Send — auto-replies to customer
Business Value
  • Replies to customers in under 5 seconds — 24/7, any time
  • Every conversation automatically recorded as a CRM lead
  • Zero human input required after setup
